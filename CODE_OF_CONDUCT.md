# Code of conduct

LibreGet follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, assume good faith, critique the work rather than the person, and do not harass anyone. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, Weblate translations, and the Telegram channel.

## Reporting

Report abusive or harassing behavior privately to [igect@vk.com](mailto:igect@vk.com). Reports are reviewed by maintainers and kept private.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block accounts that violate these rules, following the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines.
